1. Overidentified 2SLS, Effective F, LIML & Moreira CLR

Conventional first-stage F-statistics assume homoskedastic errors and overstate instrument strength under heteroskedasticity or clustering. Quasi computes the Montiel Olea & Pflueger (2013) Effective F, LIML / Fuller(α), and exact Moreira (2003) CLR weak-IV-robust hypothesis tests.

2SLS + Weak-IV Suite
import quasi

# 1. Overidentified 2SLS with HC1 robust SEs
spec = quasi.IVDesign(
    outcome="wage",
    endogenous="schooling",
    instruments=("z1", "z2"),
    covariates=("experience",),
    covariance="HC1",
)
iv_res = quasi.estimate(df_iv, design=spec)
print(iv_res.summary())

# 2. Montiel Olea & Pflueger (2013) Effective F, LIML, Fuller(1), Moreira CLR, and Hansen J
eff = iv_res.effective_f()
liml = iv_res.liml(fuller_alpha=0.0)
fuller = iv_res.liml(fuller_alpha=1.0)
clr_0 = iv_res.clr_test(null_effect=0.0)
clr_18 = iv_res.clr_test(null_effect=1.80)
j_test = iv_res.gmm_overidentification_test()

2. Just-Identified tF Intervals & Clustered IV

For single-instrument IV, .tf_confidence_interval() implements Lee, McCrary, Moreira & Porter (2022, AER) tF critical values (returning (−∞, +∞) below F = 3.8415 at α = 0.05). For clustered designs (G ≥ 10), Quasi refuses silent homoskedastic CLR and provides clustered Anderson–Rubin and opt-in clustered Hansen J tests.

tF Confidence Intervals & Clustered IV
# 3. Just-identified IV: Lee, McCrary, Moreira & Porter (2022, AER) tF Confidence Interval
iv_just = quasi.estimate(
    df_iv,
    design=quasi.IVDesign(
        outcome="wage", endogenous="schooling", instruments=("z1",),
        covariates=("experience",), covariance="HC1",
    ),
)
print(f"Standard Wald 95% CI: [{iv_just.ci_lower:.4f}, {iv_just.ci_upper:.4f}]")
print(f"Lee et al. tF 95% CI: [{iv_just.tf_confidence_interval()[0]:.4f}, {iv_just.tf_confidence_interval()[1]:.4f}]")

# 4. One-way Clustered IV (CR1, requires G >= 10) + Opt-in Clustered Hansen J
iv_cl = quasi.estimate(
    df_iv,
    design=quasi.IVDesign(
        outcome="wage", endogenous="schooling", instruments=("z1", "z2"),
        covariates=("experience",), cluster="region", covariance="CR1",
    ),
)
j_cl = iv_cl.gmm_overidentification_test(allow_clustered=True)
print(f"Clustered 2SLS (G=40): est={iv_cl.estimate:.4f}, SE={iv_cl.se:.4f}, Clustered J={j_cl.statistic:.3f} (p={j_cl.p_value:.4f})")

Estimand Honesty (No Automatic LATE Claim)
Under heterogeneous treatment effects, 2SLS estimates a Local Average Treatment Effect (LATE) only if monotonicity holds alongside relevance and exclusion. Quasi intentionally avoids labeling IV estimates as LATE without monotonicity justification.
